Why We Built KrishiMitra AI

Bridging Data Science & Smallholder Farming

Over 85% of farmers in Uttar Pradesh operate on small and marginal landholdings (under 2 hectares). Most agricultural statistics are published at the district or hectare level, making it difficult for an individual farmer with a 1.5-acre or 2-acre plot to translate regional data into practical field decisions.

KrishiMitra AI translates district-level Area, Production, and Yield (APY) records, live Open-Meteo weather telemetry, and ICAR agronomic guidelines into clear, plot-level insights in both Quintals per Acre (q/acre) and Quintals per Hectare (q/ha).

Core Platform Capabilities

Four integrated pillars designed for reliability, transparency, and farmer accessibility

🌾

1. Plot-Level Yield Engine

Calibrates district APY baselines with sowing window, irrigation source, NPK nutrition, and March heat-stress adjustments.

⛅

2. Live Open-Meteo Telemetry

Fetches real-time district temperature (°C), humidity (%), wind speed (km/h), and rainfall (mm) without simulated placeholders.

📋

3. ICAR Stage Advisories

Delivers structured recommendations for Crown Root Initiation (CRI), Yellow Rust wipe-tests, and Mustard Aphid thresholds.

🎙️

4. KrishiSathi RAG + Web Search

Searches local official scheme PDFs first, falls back to verified .gov.in portals, and supports English & Hindi voice.
