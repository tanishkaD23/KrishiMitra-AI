How KrishiMitra AI Works

Designed specifically for smallholder farmers in Western Uttar Pradesh for the Rabi season.

1
Village & Plot-Level Yield Forecast

Calibrated on 11 years of official UP district agricultural census (APY) with transparent disclaimers.

2
Scientific Stage Advisory

Crown Root Initiation (CRI) 21-day irrigation and March terminal heat risk warnings (0.5% potassium nitrate).

3
Verified Government Scheme Guidance

Instant match with PM-KISAN, PMFBY (1.5% premium), Soil Health Card, and PMKSY micro-irrigation subsidies.

4
KrishiSathi Hindi Voice AI

Speak queries naturally in Hindi and listen aloud without needing complex app navigation.
