Personalised Crop Advisories

Actionable recommendations structured by Observation, Recommended Action, Agronomic Reason, and Urgency Priority.

  • Crown Root Initiation (CRI): Critical first irrigation at 21–25 days after sowing with split urea top-dressing.
  • Disease & Pest Diagnostics: Rapid field checks for Wheat Yellow Rust and Mustard Aphids (Lipaphis erysimi).
  • Terminal Heat Mitigation: Light evening irrigation guidance when March temperatures exceed 31°C.
